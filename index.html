About me

I'm a fourth-year undergraduate in the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University. I'll join the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS) at Tsinghua University as a Ph.D. student in September 2027, advised by Prof. Tao Du.

I'm also a research intern at the Beijing Institute for General Artificial Intelligence (BIGAI), working with Dr. Yixin Chen on 3D scene reconstruction, real-to-sim, and loco-manipulation.

Previously, I interned at Galbot, advised by Dr. Zhizheng Zhang and Prof. He Wang, where I worked on world models for navigation.

  • I want to become an independent thinker and researcher, and to make a difference.
  • I aspire to create epic works of art that resonate with the world.
  • And I hope to live with discipline, purpose, and passion.
